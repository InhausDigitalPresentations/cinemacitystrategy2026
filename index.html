Where we are

Seven million views in seven months. Reach is not the problem. What we have not yet done is give those audiences a reason to choose Cinemacity over anywhere else.

The insight

People decide what to watch long before they open Instagram. What they have not decided is where. That choice is made late, usually with someone else, and nobody is arguing for it.

The idea

The room makes the movie. Sharjah’s only IMAX. Abu Dhabi’s largest screen. The country’s only licensed auditorium. Real kitchens and real bars. Cinemacity has rooms nobody else has — so we make the rooms the story.

The work

Five pillars — Movies First, Location & Experience, Entertainment & Culture, People & Moments and Creator Seeding — with fifteen recurring formats and a defined weekly volume per platform.

04The Direction

The insight

The film is already decided.
The night is not.

By the time someone opens Instagram, they know what they want to watch. Studios spend enormous sums settling that question.

The second question is wide open. Where. With whom. What kind of evening it becomes. Nearly three quarters of visits are group occasions, so it is rarely one person’s decision.

Today that choice is made on habit and proximity, because nobody in the category is arguing for it.

Cinemacity is the one brand here with something to argue with. Not scale — rooms. And food, bars and venues that do not exist anywhere else in the country.

05The Direction

Positioning

The room makes the movie

Cinemacity does not post about films. It posts about the experience films are seen in — screen, seat, food, the bar afterwards — and names it, every time.

What it means

Every post carries an argument: this one is worth choosing. The room, the format, the meal, the evening. Never just what is on.

Why only Cinemacity

A chain with hundreds of near-identical screens cannot make this claim. Cinemacity can, because its rooms are singular.

What it gives us

A first question for every brief — which room, and why? — and a content supply that never runs dry, because it does not depend on the slate.
